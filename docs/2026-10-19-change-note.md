# Change note: practice, lessons and the corner challenge

19 October 2026

**New activities.** The title now offers Practice and Challenge beside Circuit and Free drift.

- **Practice** puts you on the open lot with no timer and no score. Press R (or Reset) to start again.
- **Optional lessons** sit inside Practice: Initiate, Sustain and Transition. Transition is a marked figure of eight: slide left round the first loop, then switch and slide right.
- **Challenge** is two linked corners scored on line, angle and transition. The result explains your first mistake.

**Handling.** Practice, lessons and the challenge use a new force-based car: front and rear tyres, weight transfer and a locking handbrake. Circuit and Free drift keep the original handling, timer and scoring for now. We will only change their default after testing with players.

**Assistance.** Choose Assisted or Reduced assistance on the title.

- **Assisted** helps catch slides with automatic countersteer. On the Circuit it also follows the road and, with one thumb, lifts for corners.
- **Reduced assistance** turns all of that off.

One thumb still accelerates by itself under either setting, and a controller always uses its right trigger for gas.

**Scores.** Your existing Circuit and Free drift bests are untouched. Runs with the new handling, Reduced assistance or a controller never replace them. Challenge bests are kept separately for each combination of handling version, scoring version, course revision, assistance and controls, so changed rules never mix with old results.

**Challenge revision.** The challenge first published today had its first corner bending the opposite way to the slide it scored. That made clean runs nearly impossible. This version is `s-bend-2` with scoring `precision-2`. Results from the first version stay under their own record and are not compared with new ones.
